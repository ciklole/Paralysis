HOW TO READ THIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

63f8b1f (Nov 4, 15:03) is the bulk project upload. The city in it is
what I had already built in the editor: 40 road pieces, 43 street
lights, 24 vrbn buildings, the racing car and the bench. The agent had
never run the city builder at this point.

327d543 (Nov 4, 17:24) adds 14 more buildings to City only, by hand in
the editor, with mesh colliders on them. CarDriving is untouched.

a2e8101 (Nov 5, 21:14) is CityBuilder.BuildCity. It placed 19 house
prefabs (B00004.prefab, B00009.prefab, B00274.prefab and so on) and
then synced the driving scene, so both scenes jump to the same counts.
Its own log line is: CityBuilder: placed 19 houses (saved=True)

25a1e9b (Nov 7, 15:04) replaces those 19 prefab instances with direct
LOD_1 mesh references (Building_00024_LOD_1.fbx and friends). Same
count, different model. That happened after I sent a screenshot on
Nov 7 telling it the first attempt had used the flat prefabs instead of
the real 3D building meshes.

2859562 (Nov 10, 09:01) is DrivingSceneSyncTool.ApplyReverseSyncBatch.
I had edited CarDriving myself (16 more LOD_1 buildings, 6 more street
lights, lighting and fog) and asked the agent to copy it into City.
The copy is why the two scenes match again from here on.

4ff1cca (Nov 10, 18:17) adds 80 more street lights and 6 more buildings
that I placed by hand in the City editor that afternoon, and then the
agent ran DrivingSceneSyncTool.ApplySyncBatch to copy City into
CarDriving, which is why both scenes show the same numbers again.

The +80 street lights are named V Cone LiT (48) through V Cone LiT (127),
which is the naming Unity gives when you duplicate a light in the
editor. They are not in any grid pattern, they sit in pairs on both
sides of the pavement at irregular intervals.

CHECKING A ROW YOURSELF
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    git show 63f8b1f:Assets/Scenes/City.unity > a.txt
    git show a2e8101:Assets/Scenes/City.unity > b.txt
    (Select-String -Path a.txt,b.txt -Pattern 'm_SourcePrefab' | Measure-Object).Count

or, to see which prefab each instance points at:

    git show 4ff1cca:Assets/Scenes/City.unity |
      Select-String -Pattern 'm_SourcePrefab' | Measure-Object

To list the vrbn building models referenced by a commit:

    git show 4ff1cca:Assets/Scenes/City.unity |
      Select-String -Pattern 'Building_\d+_LOD_\d+' | Sort-Object -Unique
